# Punjab Agricultural University (PAU) Advisory Logic

## 1. Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) — The "Pani Pipe" Technology
Continuous standing water (5-7 cm flood) throughout the 120-145 days of the paddy lifecycle is an obsolete practice that creates anaerobic soil conditions and wastes immense groundwater through percolation and deep seepage.

### PAU AWD Protocol:
1. **Initial Establishment Phase**: Maintain 5 cm shallow water for only the first 15 days after transplanting to ensure root anchoring and suppress weeds.
2. **Pani Pipe Sensor Installation**:
   - Install a 40 cm long perforated PVC tube (diameter 10-15 cm) with perforation holes drilled in the lower 20 cm.
   - Insert tube 20 cm deep into the puddle soil, leaving 20 cm protruding above the soil surface.
   - Remove mud inside the tube to expose water level.
3. **Trigger for Re-irrigation**:
   - Water is applied only when the water level inside the tube drops to **5 cm below the soil surface** (-5 cm mark), or 2 days after standing ponded water has disappeared.
   - Apply only 5 cm light flood depth per irrigation.
4. **Water & Fuel Conservation**:
   - Reduces number of irrigation cycles from 28-32 down to 18-21 cycles.
   - Saves 20% to 30% of total irrigation water without compromising grain yield.

## 2. Variety Selection: PR-126 vs Pusa-44

| Parameter | Pusa-44 (Discouraged / Banned in Punjab) | PAU PR-126 (Recommended) | Net Farmer Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration (Nursery to Harvest)** | 145 - 150 days | **93 days** | **52 days saved** |
| **Total Water Depth** | ~160 cm | **~112 cm** | **48 cm water saved** |
| **Tubewell Running Hours** | ~410 hours/acre | **~270 hours/acre** | **140 hours saved/acre** |
| **Pumping Fuel / Electricity Cost**| ₹16,400/acre | **₹10,800/acre** | **₹5,600 saved/acre** |
| **Yield** | 32 - 34 quintals/acre | **30 - 32 quintals/acre** | Comparable yield |
| **Straw Generation (Parali)** | Very heavy, slow decomposer | Lower biomass, quick to rot | Easier wheat sowing via Happy Seeder |

## 3. Crop Diversification Options
In hyper-critical blocks (Dehlon, Jagraon, Raikot) where extraction exceeds 200%:
- **Kharif Maize (PMH-13)**: Requires only 45 cm irrigation water; saves 315 tubewell hours/acre compared to Pusa-44.
- **Summer Moong (SML-668 / Star-444)**: 65-day legume; consumes only 28 cm water; fixes 35-40 kg atmospheric nitrogen per hectare into soil.
