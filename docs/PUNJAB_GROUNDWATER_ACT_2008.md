# Punjab Preservation of Subsoil Water Act, 2008
*(Punjab Act No. 6 of 2008)*

## Context & Legislative Intent
Punjab's central alluvial plains extract groundwater at over 164% of natural annual recharge. The primary driver of severe summer over-draft is early paddy transplanting during the sweltering heat of May and early June, when atmospheric evapotranspiration (ET) demands exceed 8-9 mm/day.

To prevent unsustainable drawdowns in unconfined and semi-confined aquifers, the Punjab State Legislature enacted the **Punjab Preservation of Subsoil Water Act, 2008**.

## Key Statutory Provisions

### 1. Prohibition of Early Nursery Sowing (Section 3(1))
- **Statutory Mandate**: No farmer shall sow a nursery of paddy before the **10th day of May** of the agricultural year, or such other date as may be notified by the State Government in the Official Gazette.

### 2. Prohibition of Early Paddy Transplanting (Section 3(2))
- **Statutory Mandate**: No farmer shall transplant paddy before the **10th day of June** of the agricultural year (often extended to June 20th in specific notification cycles to synchronize with free agricultural power supply rosters).
- **Hydrological Benefit**: Delaying transplanting from May 15 to June 10-20 aligns the crop vegetative stage with the onset of the southwest monsoon (late June/early July), saving approximately **30 to 35 cm of irrigation water depth** (equivalent to 3-4 deep tubewell runs).

### 3. Authorized Officers & Enforcement Powers (Section 4 & 5)
- Agricultural Development Officers (ADOs), Sub-Divisional Magistrates (SDMs), and authorized district officers are legally empowered to:
  - Enter upon agricultural lands to inspect nurseries and transplanted fields.
  - Issue orders to uproot and destroy unauthorized nursery or standing crop at the farmer's expense.

### 4. Penalties (Section 6)
- **Financial Penalty**: Any farmer violating Section 3 is liable to pay a penalty of **₹10,000 per month per hectare** (or part thereof) for the duration of the violation until destroyed.
- **Conversion**: 1 Hectare ≈ 2.471 Acres.
  - For a 3-acre plot: 1.214 Hectares × ₹10,000 = **₹12,141** minimum monthly penalty.
